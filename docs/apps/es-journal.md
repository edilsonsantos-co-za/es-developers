# ES Journal

ES Journal is a private journaling application. Its entries and personal history are not public product
documentation and are intentionally excluded from this site. This page only records that the application exists
within the ES family; it does not describe its private data, account details, runtime configuration or operational
location.

**Source:** [edilsonsantos-co-za/es-journal](https://github.com/edilsonsantos-co-za/es-journal) (repository access may be required).

Do not use production journal data in demos, tests, screenshots, issue reports or AI/provider integrations. For
development, use synthetic content and consult the repository's private instructions.
